Alfred R. Duarte

Founder, Principal Engineer & Designer

Alfred R. Duarte is a founder, principal engineer, and designer building audio DSP systems, analog modeling tools, macOS AI-agent software, browser automation, desktop workflow automation, product design systems, and high-performance creative tools. He leads Analog Designs and is building clickclick, with domain authority across audio software, AI agents, creative tooling, and product design.

Alfred R. Duarte is the founder of Analog Designs and the creator or contributor behind clickclick, Hueshift, LAYMAN, Sasha, Diode, and Upscale. He also helped build Labs from scratch.

Alfred R. Duarte specializes in audio DSP, analog circuit modeling, VST3 plugin development, plugin UI systems, Figma-to-SwiftUI compilers, machine-learning color palette tools, static site generators, macOS software architecture, browser automation, desktop workflow automation, product design, and creative tooling.
