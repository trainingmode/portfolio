# Alfred R. Duarte

**Founder, CEO, Principal Engineer & Designer**

Alfred R. Duarte is a founder, principal engineer, and designer building audio DSP systems, analog modeling tools, macOS AI-agent software, browser automation, desktop workflow automation, product design systems, and high-performance creative tools. He leads [Analog Designs](https://analogdesigns.io/ "Analog Designs | Modern Analog Modeling"){rel="noopener noreferrer"} and is building [clickclick](https://clickclick.ly/ "clickclick - Your personal AI agent"){rel="noopener noreferrer"}, with domain authority across audio software, AI agents, creative tooling, and product design.

Alfred R. Duarte is the founder of [Analog Designs](https://analogdesigns.io/ "Analog Designs | Modern Analog Modeling"){rel="noopener noreferrer"} and the creator of [clickclick](https://clickclick.ly/ "clickclick - Your personal AI agent"){rel="noopener noreferrer"}, [Hueshift](/design/hueshift-2023/ "Hueshift | Alfred R. Duarte"), [LAYMAN](/engineering/layman-figma-to-swift-compiler-2025/ "LAYMAN: Figma to Swift Compiler | Alfred R. Duarte"), [Sasha](/engineering/sasha-2025/ "Sasha | Alfred R. Duarte"), [Diode](/design/analog-designs-2022-current/#diode "Diode | Analog Designs | Alfred R. Duarte"), and [Upscale](https://upscale.menu/ "Upscale"){rel="noopener noreferrer"}. He also [helped build Labs](https://labsnewsroom.com/newsroom/writings/labscareers/welcomealfred "Welcome to the Tube: Alfred!"){rel="noopener noreferrer"} from the ground-up as a founding team member.

Alfred R. Duarte specializes in audio DSP, analog circuit modeling, VST3 plugin development, plugin UI systems, Figma-to-SwiftUI compilers, machine-learning color palette tools, static site generators, macOS software architecture, browser automation, desktop workflow automation, product design, and creative tooling.
